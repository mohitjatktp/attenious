How it works

Ten minutes of play. Two minutes of parenting. One clear picture.

No cameras, no screen surveillance, no complicated setup. Just a simple weekly loop that fits real family life.

01 · PLAY

Your child plays

Ten minutes of colourful picture puzzles that adapt in real time: a right answer makes the next puzzle a little harder, a miss makes it a little easier. Every child stays in their own challenge zone — never bored, never overwhelmed.

02 · CHECK-IN

You answer a few questions

Two minutes, two or three times a week. A few smart questions about your child's day, generated from their age and recent progress. Skip a week? Attenious fills the gaps from past data — no guilt, no broken streak.

03 · REPORT

You get the report

Every week, a beautiful PDF in your inbox: attention trend, puzzle-level progress, and two or three personalised suggestions you can act on. Watch the curve climb over the months.

Questions parents ask

Good questions, honest answers.

What exactly is Attenious?

Attenious is an attention-development app for children aged 4 to 10. Kids play ten minutes of adaptive picture puzzles — puzzles get a little harder after each correct answer and a little easier after each miss, so every child stays in their personal challenge zone. Parents answer a few 2-minute questions a few times a week and receive a weekly PDF report showing their child's attention trend, progress and personalised suggestions.

Is Attenious a medical app?

No. Attenious is a skill-development and play product. It does not diagnose, treat or assess any medical condition such as ADHD. If you have concerns about your child's attention, please speak with a qualified paediatrician.

How much time does it take?

About ten minutes of puzzles for your child, and a two-minute check-in for you, two or three times a week. And if you skip a check-in, Attenious simply continues suggesting activities based on past data — no broken streaks, no guilt.

Does Attenious track my child or use the camera?

Never. Attenious does not use the camera and runs no background tracking or screen surveillance of any kind. You voluntarily share information about your child's activities, and you can delete your data at any time.

What ages is it for?

Ages 4 to 10. Questions and puzzles adapt both to your child's age and to their actual performance over time — a five-year-old and a nine-year-old get very different games from the same app.

When does it launch, and on what devices?

Attenious launches in 2027, first on Android and iOS, starting in India. Waitlist families get free early access and founding-family pricing.
